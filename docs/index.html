CropGuardNet: A Unified Lightweight CNN-Transformer Framework with Multi-Scale Attention and Explainable AI for Cross-Crop Leaf Disease Detection

Innocent Nyalala 1,2
1School of Engineering and Science, Indian Institute of Technology Madras Zanzibar, Tanzania · 2Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSDSAI), Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
Artificial Intelligence in Agriculture (Elsevier) (Nov 2026)
Code Dataset / Split CropGuard Series (1-6) BibTeX

TL;DR: CropGuardNet unifies deterministic CLAHE-Gaussian-high-pass preprocessing, a half-width MobileNetV3-Small (α = 0.50) stem, a 3-branch dilated Multi-Scale Feature Pyramid (r ∈ {1, 2, 4}), depthwise separable refinement, SE attention, a 2-head Compact ViT, dual-path pooling, and crop-aware conditioning in 481,574 parameters (1.84 MB FP32, 63.8M FLOPs, 5.00 ms CPU ONNX).

Abstract

Crop diseases threaten food security, yet deep detectors remain single-crop, heavyweight, or opaque for edge deployment. We propose CropGuardNet, a lightweight Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) and Transformer framework on MobileNetV3-Small (alpha = 0.50) with 481,574 parameters, 1.84 MB FP32 weights, and 63.8M FLOPs, running at 5.00 ms (200.0 FPS) in single-thread CPU ONNX inference. It unites a dilated multi-scale feature pyramid, depthwise separable refinement, squeeze-and-excitation attention, a two-head Transformer, dual-path pooling, crop-aware conditioning, and four explainable AI (XAI) methods: Grad-CAM++, LayerCAM, LIME, and GradientSHAP. After purging 80,238 PlantVillage duplicates via specimen Universally Unique Identifier (UUID) grouping and 64-bit perceptual hash auditing, evaluation on 35,420 unique images spanning 38 classes and 8 crops (n = 5,314 test) reaches 99.17% accuracy, 0.9874 F1-macro, 0.9914 Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC), and 0.9998 AUC-ROC, leading F1-macro across seven models at 3.2x-279.1x fewer parameters than five baselines and 6.0x larger than LeafXNet at 18.1x fewer FLOPs. Top models match on 35 non-Rice classes, whereas species conditioning eliminates cross-crop errors, supports 99.79% automatic crop routing, and lifts minority Rice accuracy to 94.44%. CropGuardNet leads 20-regime corruptions at 83.61% mean accuracy (severe Wilcoxon p = 0.0020 vs. LeafXNet), 8-fold leave-one-crop-out probing (n = 7,072) at 81.22% accuracy and 0.8879 binary AUC-ROC, and XAI faithfulness with LayerCAM Deletion AUC 0.7262 and GradientSHAP Insertion AUC 0.9259. On PlantDoc field images, guided Mode A1 reaches 40.63%-45.91% zero-shot accuracy vs. 16.06%-17.58% unguided, 60.62% unmasked vs. 62.18% masked head adaptation under Mode A1-Auto (tying ResNet-50 behind LeafXNet at 63.73%), and 62.20% Enhanced or 63.66% Raw RGB accuracy after 20-epoch tuning.

  1. Designed a compact 481,574-parameter hybrid CNN-Transformer combining parallel dilated multi-scale convolutions (r in {1, 2, 4}), depthwise separable refinement, squeeze-and-excitation recalibration, and dual-path GAP fusion at 63.8M FLOPs.
  2. Introduced a learnable 8-species crop-aware conditioning module eliminating cross-crop false positives under guided inference and supporting two-stage automatic crop routing (Mode A1-Auto) at 99.79% species accuracy.
  3. Constructed a leak-free multi-crop benchmark of 35,420 unique leaf images across 38 classes and 8 crops by purging 80,238 offline-augmented PlantVillage duplicate copies via specimen UUID grouping and auditing cross-split similarity via 64-bit DCT perceptual hashing.
  4. Evaluated four complementary XAI families (Grad-CAM++, LayerCAM, LIME, GradientSHAP) through 20-step Deletion/Insertion faithfulness curves alongside 20-regime corruption stress tests, 8-fold leave-one-crop-out probing, and external PlantDoc field adaptation.
